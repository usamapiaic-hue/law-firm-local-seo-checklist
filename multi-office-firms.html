Local SEO for Multi-Office Law Firms

Firms with more than one office face a few extra local search questions. Handled well, each office can show up for its own area. Handled badly, offices compete with each other or look like duplicates.

One profile per real office

Each staffed office can have its own Google Business Profile with its own address, phone number and hours. Do not create profiles for addresses where nobody works.

One page per office

Give each office a location page with genuinely local content: the courts nearby, directions and parking, the attorneys based there and reviews from local clients. Link each profile to its own page.

Reviews by office

Ask clients to review the office they actually used. A busy main office should not collect all the reviews while branches stay empty.

Keep details consistent

Every directory listing should match the office it describes. Mixed-up phone numbers between offices are one of the most common local SEO problems.
