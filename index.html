Law Firm Local SEO Checklist

25 checks that decide whether a firm shows up in the Google map results for "lawyer near me." By Legal Web Agency.

For most consumer law firms, the three map results above the organic listings bring more calls than any blog post. Those results depend mostly on the Google Business Profile, reviews, consistent listings and a website that confirms where the firm is and what it does. Work through these checks in order; the first section matters most. The full background is in our guide to local SEO for lawyers.

Google Business Profile (checks 1-7)

  1. The profile is claimed and verified, under the firm's real name with no extra keywords.
  2. The primary category is the most specific practice (for example "Personal injury attorney"), with relevant secondary categories.
  3. Address, phone and hours match the website exactly.
  4. Services and the business description are filled in with plain language.
  5. Real photos of the office and attorneys are uploaded.
  6. The website link points to the right page (usually the homepage or the office's location page).
  7. Questions and answers are monitored and answered.

Guide: Google Business Profile for law firms.

Reviews (checks 8-11)

  1. Every satisfied client is asked for a review, by email or text, with a direct link. Make one with the free Google review link generator.
  2. Reviews arrive steadily rather than in bursts.
  3. Every review gets a short, professional reply that does not confirm confidential details.
  4. Reviews are shown on the website correctly. See client reviews on your website and online reputation management for lawyers.

Listings and citations (checks 12-14)

  1. The firm's name, address and phone are identical on the main legal and business directories.
  2. Duplicate or outdated listings (old addresses, former partners) are merged or removed.
  3. The firm earns a few relevant local links: bar associations, local news, community sponsorships. See link building for lawyers.

Website signals (checks 15-21)

  1. The city and practice appear in the homepage title and main heading.
  2. Each office has its own location page with address, map, directions and local details.
  3. Each practice has its own page. See law firm practice area pages.
  4. LegalService or Attorney structured data includes the address and phone. See schema markup for law firms.
  5. The phone number is tappable on mobile. See mobile law firm website design.
  6. Pages load quickly on a phone. See our Core Web Vitals study of 14 law firm sites.
  7. Spanish or other language pages exist where your clients need them. See bilingual law firm websites.

Paid local options (checks 22-23)

  1. Google Local Services Ads are considered for eligible practices. See Local Services Ads for lawyers.
  2. Search ads, if used, avoid the usual budget leaks. See Google Ads mistakes law firms make.

Measurement (checks 24-25)

  1. Calls from the profile and the website are tracked separately. See conversion tracking for law firms.
  2. Rankings and calls are reviewed monthly. See what a law firm SEO report should show.

Practice-specific notes
